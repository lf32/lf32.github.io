A. Ralph Epperson published *The New World Order* in 1990 after about twenty-seven years of reading into the Great Seal, Freemasonry, and the New Age. It sits next to his earlier book *The Unseen Hand*. That one said major wars and crashes were planned. This one says the plan has a name and a logo: **Novus Ordo Seclorum** on the reverse of the U.S. Great Seal, which he translates as **The New World Order**, printed on every dollar bill.

He wrote so ordinary people could see what the symbols meant and “take corrective action.” Below are the important notes from the book, in plain English.

![Dollar bill reverse — where most people first see the seal](/images/nwo/dollar-bill-reverse.jpg)

## The central claim

Something is wrong in America: family breakdown, rising crime, pressure for world government, open borders, churches pressed by licensing. Epperson does not treat that as random decay. He says people and organizations are **deliberately** replacing the old order.

They have said so out loud:

- **Nelson Rockefeller** (1968): as president he would work toward “a new world order.”
- **Declaration of Interdependence** (1976, signed by dozens of Senators and Representatives): “now we must join with others to bring forth a new world order.”
- **Henry Kissinger**: America is uniquely placed to shape “the new order” forming around us.
- **George Bush** (1989): welcome the Soviets into the world order; a future “family of nations.”
- **Colonel Edward House** (Wilson’s advisor): World War I blood for a new world order of “peace and security.”
- **Hitler**: National Socialism would establish a new world order; more than a political party; the will to create “superman.”
- **Richard N. Gardner** in the CFR’s *Foreign Affairs* (1974): build world order from the bottom up; an **“end run around national sovereignty, eroding it piece by piece”** beats a frontal assault.

For Epperson these are not empty slogans. They are admissions.

## Who rules tomorrow

He says tomorrow’s rulers are not whoever wins the next election by accident. They are networks pushing **one-world government**, eventually under a religious-political hierarchy.

Notes he leans on:

- **Norman Cousins**: world government is “inevitable.”
- **Adam Weishaupt** founded the **Illuminati** on **May 1, 1776** and spoke of a “universal regime and empire over the whole world.”
- Strength of that Order, in Weishaupt’s own words: **concealment**. Never appear under its own name; always under another name and occupation. Hidden societies free men from the “bonds” of religion so “princes and nations shall vanish.”
- **Albert Pike** (Scottish Rite): the world will come to Masonry for its “Sovereigns” and “Pontiffs”; Masons will be “rulers over the Masters of the World”; the “Holy Empire” of Masonic brotherhood is the Royal Secret.
- **Paul Fisher**: “Masonry will eventually rule the world.”
- **Woodrow Wilson**: a power so organized and pervasive that critics should barely speak above a whisper.
- Secret orders run **two doctrines** (Pike): one for the Masters, one for the public.

## New Age, Maitreya, and the Mysteries

The big modern religious vehicle, for him, is the **New Age Movement** — not crystal kitsch, but a religion with doctrine, hierarchy, and a coming world teacher.

**Lord Maitreya** is that teacher: head of a one-world religion, holder of the “Christ” **office** (an office in a Spiritual Hierarchy of Masters, not only the historical Jesus). Alice Bailey said the Ancient Mysteries would be restored to outer life through the **Church** and the **Masonic Fraternity**. Manly P. Hall and Mackey describe the Mysteries as the secret worship of pagan gods, taught only by initiation and **symbols** so the profane never get the full story. Pike traces the Mysteries from Egypt (Osiris as sun) through Phoenicia and Greece. Masonic ritual and the Hiram Abiff legend, in this reading, come straight from Baal / Osiris / Tammuz death-and-resurrection cults.

## Symbols: serpent, sun, light, Lucifer

A long stretch of the book is symbol decoding. The chain he wants you to see:

**Serpent → “wisdom” → sun → light → Lucifer (light-bearer).**

Hall called serpent priests “Serpent Kings” and “true Sons of Light.” The serpent is divine wisdom that tempts man to know himself. Pike and Mackey treat sun worship as the oldest major religion; the lodge faces **East** (source of light); the **North** is darkness (he links that to Isaiah’s Lucifer seeking the north). Osiris is the sun; altar lights stand for Osiris, Isis, Horus. Public sun worship, he argues, was a cover for a god ordinary people would reject if named plainly: **Lucifer**.

Pike’s *Morals and Dogma*, Blavatsky’s Theosophy, and other high-degree writers are quoted to show Lucifer as intelligence and liberator, not as a metaphor. “Ye shall be as gods” becomes curriculum: man becomes divine through initiation. If man is god, no higher law binds the planners.

Epperson is careful on one point: he is **not** saying every rank-and-file Mason or New Ager knowingly worships evil. He is saying the **inner literature** and the **social program** (anti-church, anti-family, anti-nation, anti-property) line up.

## Pyramid, obelisks, and the Great Seal

![Giza](/images/nwo/giza-pyramids.jpg)

The Great Pyramid is, in occult sources he uses, an initiation house. An unfinished pyramid means the work is incomplete until the illuminated **capstone** (the All-Seeing Eye) is set.

![Washington Monument](/images/nwo/washington-monument.jpg)

Obelisks are solar monuments from the same stream, moved into modern capitals so people live among the symbols without reading them. The Washington Monument is the American example he wants you to notice.

![Great Seal reverse](/images/nwo/great-seal-reverse.png)

![Great Seal obverse](/images/nwo/great-seal-obverse.png)

The **Great Seal** is the keystone of the whole book:

- **Unfinished pyramid** — ages of work still unfinished until the New Order.
- **All-Seeing Eye / floating capstone** — not only “Providence” in schoolbooks; for him, the Mystery god completing the structure.
- **Annuit Coeptis** — Providence has favored our undertakings (he reads “our” as the secret project).
- **Novus Ordo Seclorum** — The New World Order, not vague patriotic poetry.
- **MDCCLXXVI (1776)** on the base — U.S. independence **and** the year the Illuminati was founded (May 1, 1776).
- Putting that reverse on the **dollar** means the destiny is advertised every day in every wallet.
- The eagle side (*E Pluribus Unum*, arrows, olive branch, stars) gets Masonic number talk; designers with lodge ties, he claims, knew what they were doing.

## Illuminati, Marx, and Hitler

**Weishaupt’s program** (as Epperson stresses it): destroy Christianity and national governments; control schools, pulpits, and professorial chairs; win the people “chiefly by means of the schools.” Even after Bavaria suppressed the Order, he says the **method** lived on in revolutions, communism, and elite networks.

**Karl Marx** is presented as more than an economist: anti-God at the root. The *Communist Manifesto* planks he keeps returning to are abolition of private property, centralization, progressive taxes, and **free education in public schools**. Communism is the political machine that finishes what Illuminism started against church, family, and property.

**Hitler** is not filed as ordinary right-wing politics. Epperson quotes him on the new world order, “superman,” and National Socialism as more than a party — occult-socialist total state as another brand of the same new-order impulse.

## Freemasonry from the inside of his notes

Blue lodge is the outer brotherhood. Higher degrees and Pike’s *Morals and Dogma* carry the real doctrine. The **Hiram** drama is a death-and-rebirth initiation. Obligations enforce secrecy so millions never discuss the inner meaning. The **33°** Supreme Council of the Southern Jurisdiction calls itself the Mother Council of the world; its magazine was literally titled ***The New Age***. Objectors (Catholic, Protestant, Robison-type exposés) were not bigots in Epperson’s view; they recognized a rival religion wearing a charity pin.

## Humanism, situation ethics, and the social targets

**Humanism** is the respectable face: man as measure, no Creator, international authority, socialized economics. He keeps grouping **Humanists / Masons / Communists / Illuminists / New Agers** as different labels, same destination.

**Situation ethics** dissolves fixed right and wrong. If everything is “context,” any means becomes available for the Order’s ends.

Then the institutional targets:

**Religion** — free exercise replaced by licensed, managed faith. Soviet law is his warning chart: churches registered, youth Bible groups banned, school separated from church, atheistic education as state policy. American cases of home-school arrests and church fines for unlicensed ministries are, for him, the same path starting.

**Family** — rival power center (loyalty, teaching, property). Divorce, sexual revolution, and state displacement of parents atomize people so a world government faces individuals, not households.

**Education** — Weishaupt, Marx, Stalin (via Cvetic’s FBI undercover notes), Khrushchev, and Komsomol all treat youth capture as essential. Public school monopoly versus home and private Christian schooling is the battlefield. “To submit [to state licensing of church schools] is to admit that the state is lord over the church.”

**Liberty** — redefined toward mandatory service and controlled association. When “liberty” means compulsion, he says the New World Order is close.

**Property** — Disraeli’s note that secret societies want to change land tenure and end ecclesiastical establishments. Without property, rights become permissions from the state.

**Nationalism** — borders and patriotism block one-world rule; Gardner’s “piece by piece” erosion is the method. “Family of nations” talk softens the loss of independence.

## Reagan, Bush, and “eleven short years”

Epperson’s late chapters try to pin the abstract plan to late-1980s politics.

- April 1988 *New Age Magazine* (Scottish Rite 33°) cover: **Reagan** with the top Scottish Rite and Shrine leaders and a framed certificate.
- 1988 GOP convention: Reagan on the “new millennium,” a “new age,” a “**sunlit** new day” (sun = Lucifer light in his symbol system).
- Bush’s “**thousand points of light**” — he stresses the jump from countless volunteer groups to exactly a thousand, and “light” as lodge light; a thousand years as the Maitreya millennium.
- Bush, March 1989: prepare for “the new world” coming in “**eleven short years**” → year **2000**.
- Millennium Society party planned at the **Great Pyramid of Giza** for 1999–2000; Bush telegram looking forward to Egypt in 1999. Epperson reads that as ritual timing for the New Age, not a tourist gala.

![Giza again](/images/nwo/great-pyramid.jpg)

He knows skeptics will laugh. His answer is basically: hold judgment until you stack the seal, the literature, the policy targets, and the political language together.

## How he sums it up, and what he wants done

Proudhon’s line is his one-sentence NWO: **atheism in religion, anarchy in politics, no property in the economic sphere.** Orwell’s *1984* image is the emotional closer: a boot stamping on a human face forever.

On the way out he quotes Solzhenitsyn (“Men have forgotten God; that’s why all this has happened”), de Tocqueville (America is great because she is good; when she ceases to be good she ceases to be great), and **II Chronicles 7:14** — humble yourselves, pray, seek God, turn from wicked ways, and He will heal the land. The “solution” chapter is spiritual and collective resistance, not a policy white paper.

People asked why he was still alive after *The Unseen Hand*. He had no sure answer. He named Lincoln, Forrestal, McCarthy, McFadden, and reporter Don Bolles as men who got too close. His pre-death note: if his car “explodes” or he “falls” from a high building, he did not do it — double the effort to expose the conspiracy.

## What the book is really saying, compressed

The New World Order, in Epperson’s notes, is all of this at once:

- One-world **government** (sovereignty eroded gradually)
- One-world **religion** under Maitreya / Hierarchy
- Mystery religion restored through Masonry and captured churches
- Inner **Lucifer / sun / serpent light** doctrine for adepts
- Illuminati **secrecy and school capture**
- Socialist **property** abolition and central control
- Humanism and situation ethics as public morality
- War on biblical faith, family, private education, property, and nationalism
- The **U.S. Great Seal reverse** as America’s secret-destiny mark
- Late-century “light / millennium / Giza” politics as a near-term signpost (from a 1990 horizon)

You do not have to accept every link. The useful work the book still does is force attention onto the primary esoteric texts, the seal language on the money, and how education, family, property, and religion move together when nation-states thin out.
